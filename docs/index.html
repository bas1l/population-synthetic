Sweden Generation Explorer

How a Swedish individual is generated by conditional chained sampling: a per-individual generation DAG plus a by-output-category view of the candidate SCB source tables with live distribution slicing.

Comparison Metrics — Field Guide

The metrics used to score synthetic vs. real populations, each explained end to end — TV distance, KL divergence, Cramér’s V, coherence, combination plausibility, grounded joint TV — with an interactive C2ST walkthrough and ten deep-dive notes.

Open field guide → architecture/comparison-metrics-report.html Research atlas

LLM Comparison Methods — Atlas

How do we tell one language model from another? An interactive literature atlas over the methods for measuring LLM precision and accuracy — a citation graph, a categorized bibliography, and strategy notes.

Open atlas → research/llm-comparison-methods/ Reference
