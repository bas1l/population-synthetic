# SCB PxWeb Table Catalog — Labour Market & Education

A live dump of **every table** under the **AM** (labour market) and **UF** (education)
subject areas of the [SCB PxWeb API](https://api.scb.se/OV0104/v1/doris/en/ssd), captured
with full variable/value metadata. Built while investigating whether SCB exposes
**labour-force status broken down by age** (and by age × education) for the Swedish
population generator.

## Key finding

**No SCB PxWeb table combines labour-force status × education × a genuine age band.**
The only table carrying all three variable *names* — `AM/AM0210/AM0210A/ArbStatusUtbM` —
degrades its age dimension to the working-age totals (`20–64 / 20–65 / 20–66`), so it
cannot slice status × education by a real age band. The available options are:

- **status × age (5-yr bands) × sex** — `AM/AM0210/AM0210D/ArRegArbStatus` (register, annual)
  or `AM/AM0401/AM0401A/AKURLBefAr` (AKU survey). Both cap at age 74.
- **status × education × sex** (no real age) — `AM/AM0401/AM0401P/NAKUBefUtbNivAr`.
